Pretrained PP-OCRv4 models are distributed by RapidAI/RapidOCR 3.4.2,
derived from PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR mobile OCR models (Apache-2.0).

RapidOCR source and model table:
https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR/tree/v3.4.2
https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR/blob/v3.4.2/python/rapidocr/default_models.yaml

PaddleOCR upstream:
https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR

Runtime model files and recognition dictionary are checksum-pinned in
app/services/document_ocr.py. The original Apache-2.0 licence is included as
LICENSE-RapidOCR.txt. No identity documents or evaluation samples are bundled.
