A real case

D900,000 left a wallet. Someone changed the SIM and the PIN.

In 2022 an Africell staff member was charged in Kanifing. The charge says he changed customers’ SIMs and PINs, and moved their Afrimoney. He pleaded not guilty.

Today

People in The Gambia already use a wallet.

Wave is the one most people use. In 2023 it handled about 45 percent of wallet payments. Afrimoney was next. People also cash in and cash out with an agent.

The problem

Two things go wrong.

1 Signup does not check the person.

On Wave you can upload your ID later. On Afrimoney you type an ID number. The wallet can still pay. Change the SIM and the PIN, and the money can move. That is the Kanifing charge.

2 The balance is only a number.

If a payment is sent twice, or stops halfway, that number can be wrong. There is no list of records to show what the balance should be.

The solution

Sutura is the wallet. Sentinel is the check.

Sutura A wallet that keeps every payment.

You send, receive, cash in, and cash out. Each payment is written as two matching records. The balance is those records added up.

Sentinel A check before the wallet pays.

Sentinel asks: is this the real person, and is this payment safe? It answers allow, review, or block, and it writes the reason.

The customer uses the Sutura wallet. The money stays there. Sentinel never holds it.

Sutura

What the wallet does.

  • A customer opens a wallet, sends money, and receives money.
  • An agent takes cash in, or pays cash out.
  • A shop collects with a payment link.
  • An admin can freeze a wallet, or undo a payment.
  • Each action is saved as two matching records. The balance is those records added up.

Sutura

You cash in D1,000. The wallet writes it twice.

  1. 1The cash box goes up by D1,000.
  2. 2Your wallet goes up by D1,000.
  3. 3If the two amounts do not match, the payment is refused.
  4. 4If the same payment is sent again, it is refused.
  5. 5The number on the screen is the sum of these records.

Sutura

She sends D200 to another wallet.

  1. 1Her wallet goes down by D200.
  2. 2The other wallet goes up by D200.
  3. 3Both lines are one payment. If the amounts differ, it is refused.
  4. 4If the phone sends it twice, the second copy is refused.
  5. 5Each wallet balance is the sum of its own records.

Sentinel

Two checks. The person, then the payment.

KYC. Is this the real person?

You show your ID and your face. Sentinel checks that the face matches, and that a live person is there. A weak result stops the account. Wave lets you upload the ID later. Afrimoney takes an ID number.

Fraud. Is this payment safe?

Before each payment, Sentinel looks at the amount, the time, and who is paid. Allow, review, or block. The reason names what triggered it. Staff must write a note.

Sentinel

Three answers. The money follows.

  1. AllowThe wallet pays. Sutura writes the two records, and the balance changes.
  2. ReviewThe money waits. Staff read the reason and write a note. Then Sutura pays or stops.
  3. BlockThe wallet does not pay. No record is saved. If Sentinel cannot be reached, the wallet also does not pay.

The project

One customer, from signup to payment.

  1. 1She opens a Sutura wallet and shows her ID. Sentinel checks that she is there.
  2. 2She gives an agent D1,000. Sutura writes the cash box up, and her wallet up, by the same amount.
  3. 3She sends D200. Sentinel says allow. Sutura pays, and writes that payment twice.
  4. 4A later payment looks unsafe. Sentinel says review. The money waits. Staff read the reason and write a note.
  5. 5If that payment is sent again, Sutura refuses the copy. If Sentinel does not answer, the wallet does not pay.

Two screens

The customer stays in the wallet.

Sutura

The customer, the agent, the shop, and the admin. Sending, cash in, cash out, and payment links all happen here. The balance changes only here.

Sentinel

Staff only. They see the case, the reason for allow, review, or block, and they must write a note. They cannot pay, and they cannot change the balance. The customer has no Sentinel account.

Sutura asks Sentinel when the wallet is opened, and again before each payment.

The staff screen

What staff do with a case.

  • A payment on review opens a case.
  • They see the amount, the person, and the reason for the score.
  • They decide, and they must write a note. A decision with no note is refused.
  • The screen keeps who decided, and when.
  • Sutura then pays, stops, or keeps waiting. Staff never type a new balance.

How I will know

I will try to break both sides.

  • A payment whose two sides do not match is refused.
  • The same payment sent twice is saved once.
  • An agent cannot undo a customer’s wallet. A customer cannot open the staff screen.
  • A weak face check does not pass. A payment that looks unlike the customer’s usual pattern is held, and the reason is shown.
  • One full run: open a wallet, cash in, allow one payment, hold another, and show the records and the case.
